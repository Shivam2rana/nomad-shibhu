Tungnath & Chandrashila

Panch KedarTreksRudraprayag district

My experience

The highest Shiva temple in the world, and a short climb on to Chandrashila for a sunrise over Chaukhamba, Nanda Devi and Trishul.

The full story from this trip is coming soon. The reels are already up on Instagram.

Day-by-day itinerary

Starts and ends in Rishikesh. Getting to Rishikesh is on you. How to reach

  1. Day 1

    Rishikesh to Chopta

    Meet in Rishikesh in the morning. Drive about 200 km via Devprayag, Rudraprayag and Ukhimath. Stay in Chopta's camps or homestays.

  2. Day 2

    Sunrise trek to Chandrashila

    Start before dawn, reach Tungnath (3.5 km), continue 1.5 km to Chandrashila for sunrise, then come down for darshan. Rest in Chopta.

  3. Day 3

    Chopta to Rishikesh

    Drive back, reaching Rishikesh by evening.

Tips before you go

  • Leave Chopta by 4:30 am if you want the Chandrashila sunrise.
  • In winter the temple closes and the deity moves to Makkumath.
