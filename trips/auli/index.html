Auli & Gorson Bugyal

TreksChamoli district

My experience

Snow slopes in winter and open meadows in summer, with a walk up to Gorson Bugyal and Nanda Devi across the valley.

The full story from this trip is coming soon. The reels are already up on Instagram.

Day-by-day itinerary

Starts and ends in Rishikesh. Getting to Rishikesh is on you. How to reach

  1. Day 1

    Rishikesh to Auli

    Drive up the Alaknanda via Rudraprayag and Chamoli to Joshimath, then the last stretch up to Auli.

  2. Day 2

    Auli and Gorson Bugyal

    Walk about 3 km through oak and conifer forest to the Gorson Bugyal meadow for views of Nanda Devi and Dronagiri. Back in Auli by afternoon.

  3. Day 3

    Auli to Rishikesh

    Drive back, reaching Rishikesh by evening.

Tips before you go

  • Come in January or February for snow; ski gear can be hired in Auli.
  • Easy to pair with Badrinath or Valley of Flowers, which are on the same highway past Joshimath.
