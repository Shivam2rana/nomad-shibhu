Satopanth Tal Trek

TreksChamoli district

My experience

A tough high-altitude walk from Mana along the Alaknanda's glacier valley to a triangular sacred lake under Chaukhamba and Neelkanth.

The full story from this trip is coming soon. The reels are already up on Instagram.

Day-by-day itinerary

Starts and ends in Rishikesh. Getting to Rishikesh is on you. How to reach

  1. Day 1

    Rishikesh to Joshimath

    Drive up the Alaknanda valley to Joshimath.

  2. Day 2

    Joshimath to Badrinath

    Short drive to Badrinath. Darshan, then rest and acclimatise.

  3. Day 3

    Mana to Laxmi Van

    Walk from Mana past Vasudhara falls to the Laxmi Van campsite.

  4. Day 4

    Laxmi Van to Chakratirtha

    Cross moraine and boulder fields past Sahastradhara to Chakratirtha.

  5. Day 5

    Satopanth Tal

    Steep climb to the lake (about 4,600 m) under Chaukhamba, then back to Chakratirtha.

  6. Day 6

    Back to Badrinath

    Long walk down to Mana and Badrinath.

  7. Day 7

    Badrinath to Rishikesh

    Long drive back, reaching Rishikesh late in the evening.

Tips before you go

  • This trek is above 4,000 m on unmarked moraine; do it only with prior high-altitude experience.
  • Check permit rules with the forest office in Joshimath or Badrinath before you go, and take a registered local guide.
  • Nights at Chakratirtha are below freezing even in summer; carry a proper sleeping bag.
