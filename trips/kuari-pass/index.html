Kuari Pass Trek

TreksChamoli district

My experience

Lord Curzon's old trail through meadows and forest to a pass facing Nanda Devi, Dronagiri, Kamet and Hathi Ghoda in one sweep.

The full story from this trip is coming soon. The reels are already up on Instagram.

Day-by-day itinerary

Starts and ends in Rishikesh. Getting to Rishikesh is on you. How to reach

  1. Day 1

    Rishikesh to Joshimath

    Drive up the Alaknanda past the five prayags to Joshimath.

  2. Day 2

    Dhak to Gulling

    Short drive to Dhak, then climb through villages and forest to the Gulling campsite.

  3. Day 3

    Gulling to Khullara

    Walk through oak forest to the Khullara meadows.

  4. Day 4

    Kuari Pass

    Climb to the pass (about 3,815 m) for the full Garhwal panorama, then return to camp.

  5. Day 5

    Down to Auli

    Descend via Tali through the forest to Auli, then on to Joshimath.

  6. Day 6

    Joshimath to Rishikesh

    Drive back, reaching Rishikesh by evening.

Tips before you go

  • Winter brings snow from about Khullara upwards; summer turns the meadows green.
  • Combine with a day in Auli or a visit to Badrinath if you have extra days.
