Valley of Flowers

ValleysTreksChamoli district

My experience

A UNESCO-listed alpine valley that turns into a carpet of wildflowers in the monsoon, with Hemkund Sahib as an optional climb.

The full story from this trip is coming soon. The reels are already up on Instagram.

Day-by-day itinerary

Starts and ends in Rishikesh. Getting to Rishikesh is on you. How to reach

  1. Day 1

    Rishikesh to Govindghat

    Drive to Govindghat near Joshimath.

  2. Day 2

    Trek to Ghangaria

    Shared jeep to Pulna, then about 10 km up the Lakshman Ganga to Ghangaria.

  3. Day 3

    Valley of Flowers

    Walk into the valley (about 4 km in) and spend the day among the flowers. Return to Ghangaria by evening.

  4. Day 4

    Descend to Govindghat

    Trek back to Pulna. Add a day here if you want the steep climb to Hemkund Sahib.

  5. Day 5

    Govindghat to Rishikesh

    Drive back, reaching Rishikesh by evening.

Tips before you go

  • The park is open 1 June to 31 October. Entry is 7 am to 2 pm and everyone must be out by 5 pm.
  • The permit (about ₹150 for Indians, valid three days) is available online or at the forest counter in Ghangaria. Camping inside is not allowed.
  • Pack a proper rain jacket; this is a monsoon trek.
