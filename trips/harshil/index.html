Harshil Valley

ValleysUttarkashi district

My experience

A deodar-and-apple valley on the Bhagirathi, with Bagori village and Mukhba, the winter home of Goddess Ganga.

The full story from this trip is coming soon. The reels are already up on Instagram.

Day-by-day itinerary

Starts and ends in Rishikesh. Getting to Rishikesh is on you. How to reach

  1. Day 1

    Rishikesh to Harshil

    Drive via Uttarkashi up the Bhagirathi valley and settle into a riverside stay.

  2. Day 2

    Bagori and Mukhba

    Walk through Bagori village and the orchards, then visit Mukhba across the river.

  3. Day 3

    Harshil to Rishikesh

    Drive back, reaching Rishikesh by evening.

Tips before you go

  • Pair it with Gangotri, which is only about an hour further up the road.
