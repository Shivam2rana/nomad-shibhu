Badrinath & Mana

Char DhamChamoli district

My experience

The Char Dham shrine of Vishnu on the Alaknanda between the Nar and Narayan ranges, with Mana village a few kilometres on.

The full story from this trip is coming soon. The reels are already up on Instagram.

Day-by-day itinerary

Starts and ends in Rishikesh. Getting to Rishikesh is on you. How to reach

  1. Day 1

    Rishikesh to Joshimath

    Drive past the five prayags along the Alaknanda.

  2. Day 2

    Badrinath and Mana

    Dip in Tapt Kund, darshan, then Mana village, Bhim Pul and the Saraswati river. Stay in Joshimath.

  3. Day 3

    Joshimath to Rishikesh

    Drive back, reaching Rishikesh by evening.

Tips before you go

  • Register online for the Char Dham yatra before you travel (registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in). It is checked at the shrine.
  • Phones and cameras are not allowed inside the temple premises, so film outside.
